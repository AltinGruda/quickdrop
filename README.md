# QuickDrop

**Send files from your phone to your computer. No app. No cables. No cloud.**

QuickDrop runs on your Mac or Windows PC, shows a QR code, and waits. Open your phone's camera, scan it, pick your files — and they land on your computer, complete and verified.

| Platform | Download |
|----------|----------|
| **Windows 10 / 11** | [QuickDrop Setup.exe](https://github.com/AltinGruda/quickdrop/releases/latest/download/QuickDrop-setup.exe) |
| **macOS** | [QuickDrop.dmg](https://github.com/AltinGruda/quickdrop/releases/latest/download/QuickDrop.dmg) |

## How it works

1. Open QuickDrop on your computer — a QR code appears.
2. Scan it with your phone's camera (no app needed).
3. Pick photos, videos, anything. They arrive with a live progress bar.
4. Done. Files are in your Downloads folder.

If a transfer drops out — phone locks, Wi-Fi stutters — QuickDrop picks up where it left off. Files only appear when they're 100% complete and checksum-verified.

## Requirements

- Phone and computer on the **same home Wi-Fi** network.
- QuickDrop works on Mac and Windows. Your phone needs a browser — that's it.

## Honest notes

- **Home Wi-Fi only.** Hotel, office, and public guest networks usually won't work.
- **QR code is the door.** Anyone who scans it while the session is open can send files. Close it when you're done.
- **One way.** Phone to computer. That's it.
- **No encryption.** Everything stays on your local network. Nothing touches the internet.
