No more photo-transfer workarounds

Most people end up stuck with awkward ways to move photos. Here's each of them, and why QuickDrop simply skips the problem:

  • Emailing yourself. Upload, then download, then delete the draft. Slow, and it fills your inbox.
  • Wires and cables. The right cable, the right port, the right "mode." Guesswork every time.
  • Cloud uploads. An account, a slow re-download, and often a squeezed version of your photo.
  • Share apps. An app on both your phone and your computer, plus a discovery step that can fail.

QuickDrop needs none of that: it's one code, one tap, and the originals land on your computer over your home Wi-Fi.

Frequently asked questions

Will my photos be compressed?

No. Files keep their original quality and names. Nothing is re-encoded.

Can I send many photos at once?

Yes. Pick as many as you like in one go. QuickDrop shows live progress for every file.

Where do they end up?

In your computer's Downloads folder. Nothing is overwritten, and a file only appears once it's fully transferred.

Does this need an internet connection?

No, just a home Wi-Fi network. Your phone and computer talk directly to each other.

Do I need an app on my phone?

No. Your phone just opens a normal browser page when you scan the code.
