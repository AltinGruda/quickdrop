One folder. Nothing lost.

Made for home Wi-Fi, not hotel Wi-Fi

Hotel, office, and public guest networks isolate devices from each other, so QuickDrop usually will not work on them. If files will not move on a network like that, try a personal hotspot instead.

Privacy

Your files never touch the internet.

Transfer happens over your home Wi-Fi, straight from the phone to this computer. No cloud, no account, nothing saved anywhere else. When the window closes, the session is gone.

Nothing is sent to us

There is no QuickDrop server to send anything to. It all stays on your two devices, on your own network.

Nothing left behind

An unfinished transfer never leaves a stray file on your computer, and old session files clean themselves up.

Honest about limits

Sessions are not encrypted or private. Anyone who has the code while it is live can send files. Use it face to face on your own Wi-Fi, and close it when you’re done.
