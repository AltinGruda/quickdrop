Privacy & Terms

Last updated: September 3, 2026

QuickDrop is a small desktop app that moves files from your phone to your computer over your own home Wi‑Fi. This page explains a simple, honest policy: what the app and website do and don’t collect, and the rules for using them.

Privacy Policy

The short version

QuickDrop is built around the idea that files should stay between your own devices. It has no cloud, no accounts, no servers, and we don’t collect or sell anything. Below are the specific details.

File transfers stay local

When you use the app, files go directly from your phone to your computer over your local network (usually your home Wi‑Fi). They are not uploaded to any QuickDrop service, not stored by us, and not routed through the internet. When a transfer is complete, the file lives on your computer and nowhere else.

What the app collects

Nothing, by design. The app does not create an account, does not phone home, and collects no usage data, analytics, or personal information. There is no telemetry.

What the website collects

  • Color theme preference. If you switch between the light and dark look of this website, that single setting is stored locally in your browser. It is not sent to us.
  • Email address (only if you subscribe). If you use the optional email signup on the homepage, we receive the email address you enter and we use it only to send you updates about QuickDrop. You can unsubscribe at any time, and we never rent or sell your address.

Honest limitations

File sessions are not encrypted. Because a transfer is opened by scanning a code, anyone who has that code while the session is live can send files. We recommend using QuickDrop face to face on a network you trust, and closing the session when you’re done.

Children

QuickDrop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on the QuickDrop GitHub repository.
